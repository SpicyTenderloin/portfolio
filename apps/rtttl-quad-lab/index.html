    Select a note

    Click a channel above to switch it on or off; channels that are on are overlaid in their own colour, and the one marked ✎ is the one you're editing (the last one you switched on). Click or drag another channel's note to start editing that channel. Drag a note to move it or change pitch; drag its striped right edge to resize. Without Ripple editing, other notes stay where they are: you can slide a note past its neighbours or stack two notes at the same time, and empty space becomes rests. A motor can only play one note at a time, so overlapping notes get a red outline and the RTTTL below keeps the last version without overlaps until you fix them. Enable Ripple editing to shift later notes when adjusting the timing. Lengths are fractions of a whole note (1/4 is one beat). Double-click to insert a note, or use + Add note to put an identical copy of the selected note right after it; select a note and press Delete to remove it. Shift- or Ctrl-click notes to select several (Ctrl + A selects all) and drag any of them to move the group. Ctrl + C / Ctrl + X copy or cut the selection (copying moves the playhead to just after it); click an empty spot or the bar ruler to place the playhead, then Ctrl + V pastes there, into whichever channel you're editing. Ctrl + Z undoes and Ctrl + Y (or Ctrl + Shift + Z) redoes. Choose a Key to highlight its notes on the keyboard and grid. End pause sets the silence after the song's last note, counted from whichever channel ends latest: up to the end of the bar, up to the end of the quarter bar, none, or a custom length entered as a number of notes (for example 5 × 1/4 note). Every channel gets the rest it needs so all the ESCs finish together. With Arming beeps on (Sound panel), tick Arming beep on the beat to end the melody just early enough that the first beep lands on that bar line, quarter-bar line or beat (None: the next beat; Custom: that length after the last note); this is kept up to date as you edit in the piano roll, and changing it (or pressing Apply) updates every channel. Zoom with the Time and Pitch buttons (or Ctrl + mouse wheel for time, Ctrl + Shift + mouse wheel for pitch), Fit to show the whole melody, or Full screen for more room. Play / Pause above the grid plays from the white playhead; click the bar ruler to move it. Clicking a note plays it, and dragging it up or down plays each new pitch; click a key on the left to hear that pitch. Dashed mint notes are automatic pitch suggestions (not yet applied); click one to hear it. The RTTTL below stays synchronised.
